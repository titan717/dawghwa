# LENS cycle 1: competitor patterns and feature backlog

Owner: LENS (competitor and metrics analyst). Status: proposal for the lead to triage. Docs only; nothing here is built.

This extends [DAWGHWA_RESEARCH.md](DAWGHWA_RESEARCH.md), [UX_GAMIFICATION_AUDIT.md](UX_GAMIFICATION_AUDIT.md) and [DECISIONS.md](DECISIONS.md). It does not repeat what those already cover (hero, genre shelf, title page CTA, RTL cue, reading statuses, mature blur).

## Method and rules

- Only public pages were read: official sites, app store listings, help centres, public repos and changelogs, and a few press or news pages where noted. Every pattern links to the page it was seen on.
- Patterns are described in our own words. No text, markup, assets, colours or branding are copied, and none should be copied when building.
- "Third-party" marks a claim that comes from a news or blog page rather than the product itself. "Unverified" marks something the page suggests but does not fully confirm (for example an open pull request that may not have shipped).
- Pages were read on 22 Nov 2026. Products change; recheck a link before building against it.

### Note on Comick

The brief asked for Comick. comick.io went offline on 16 Sep 2025 and was reported as an unlicensed manga site ([CBR](https://www.cbr.com/manga-piracy-comick-shutdown/), third-party). The App Store app now called "Comick: Comic & eBook Reader" is a different product, a local-file ebook and comic reader ([App Store](https://apps.apple.com/in/app/comick-comic-ebook-reader/id6479202713)). So there's no live, legitimate Comick product to study, and the row for Comick in [DAWGHWA_RESEARCH.md](DAWGHWA_RESEARCH.md) describes a site that no longer exists. Recommendation: leave the old row as history, add a dated note, and use Kotatsu and Suwayomi as the dense-library references in future cycles. Nothing in the backlog below depends on Comick.

## Findings that bear on Dawghwa's fixed decisions

1. Weekday schedules go stale. WEBTOON's Originals list is grouped by weekday plus a Completed tab ([WEBTOON Originals](https://www.webtoons.com/en/originals)). A third-party check found that three of six big series were on hiatus while their pages still named a weekday ([ChapterBrief](https://www.chapterbrief.net/blog/what-time-does-webtoon-update), third-party). This supports the existing rule: weekday tabs only where release data is trustworthy, otherwise a "New this week" row built from real chapter dates.
2. A "what came out recently" feed is a separate surface from browsing. MANGA Plus groups the latest chapters under "last 24 hours" and then by date, each with a direct "read latest" action ([MANGA Plus Updates](https://mangaplus.shueisha.co.jp/updates)). Mihon's Updates tab does the same for saved titles (bottom bar described by a [third-party Mihon guide](https://tachiyomiguide.com/mihon-app-guide/)).
3. Ranks show movement, not only position. WEBTOON's ranking lists mark each entry as up or down by N places, and new entries get a NEW tag ([WEBTOON rankings, mobile](https://m.webtoons.com/en/ranking/canvas)). That pairs well with the big ranked numerals already in Dawghwa, but only if we keep yesterday's ranks on the device.
4. "Not for me" is expected on recommendation rows. WEBTOON added a way to take a series out of its personal picks row in Aug 2025 ([Comics Beat](https://www.comicsbeat.com/webtoon-rolls-out-two-new-qol-features-to-their-platform/), third-party). Dawghwa already has Not interested and Hide; the gap is making them reachable from the row itself.
5. Onboarding by genre and theme feeds personal picks at WEBTOON too ([WEBTOON press release](https://about.webtoon.com/press-release/183)), so Dawghwa's onboarding is in line with the market. Their recommender is server-side; ours stays on the device.
6. History pausing exists as a named mode. Mihon calls it Incognito mode and it pauses reading history ([Mihon Library FAQ](https://mihon.app/docs/faq/library)). This is the pattern HEARTH needs.
7. Streak freezes elsewhere are bought with a currency. Duolingo's freeze must be bought in advance with gems ([Duolingo Help](https://www.duolingo.com/help/what-is-a-streak)). Dawghwa rules out coins and paid currency, so freezes have to be earned or granted on a schedule, never bought.
8. Every licensed competitor monetises with timers, passes or coins (WEBTOON Daily Pass and coins in the [Help Centre](https://help.line.me/LINE_WEBTOON/web?lang=en), Tapas Wait-Until-Free and Ink on [Google Play](https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=com.tapastic&hl=en_US), Manta Free Pass and gems on [Google Play](https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=net.manta.comic&hl=en-IN)). None of that applies to Dawghwa. Their "come back tomorrow" loops are the thing our gamification must not imitate.
